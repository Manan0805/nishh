HOW TO ADD YOUR 3 PERMANENT PHOTOS:

Copy your photos into this "public" folder with these names:

1. photo1.jpg (or photo.jpg)
   -> Shows in Section 3: "03.10.2026" (That peaceful cab ride)
   -> Default if missing: Cozy night cab-window bokeh & rain illustration

2. photo2.jpg
   -> Shows in Section 5: "It's the little things" (Unscripted & candid moments)
   -> Default if missing: Cozy coffee & polaroid camera illustration

3. photo3.jpg
   -> Shows in Section 7: "Happy Birthday Finale" (To the cutest birthday girl)
   -> Default if missing: Birthday candles & blooming floral illustration

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp (just rename the extension to .jpg or keep as .jpg)

NOTE: You can also click the "Add photo" button right on any Polaroid frame in the browser to preview them instantly!
